# ForkCast video: narration (Andrew)

For **OMARSCRIPT** ([OMARSCRIPT.md](OMARSCRIPT.md)). Omar tells his own story; you're the narrator for the product half. Read each line as its own recording. The time marks show where each line plays.

## How to record
- Quiet small room (a closet or a bedroom with curtains). No kitchens or bathrooms.
- Phone voice recorder, a hand's width from your mouth, a little off to the side.
- 2–3 takes per line, a couple of seconds apart. Keep the best.
- Clear and confident, a little faster than Omar. Smile on A-08.
- Name the files `A-01`, `A-02`, … and send them to Viktor.

**A-02** · 1:19
