# ForkCast video: narration script (Andrew)

You're the narrator. Maya is a fictional character, so speak about her warmly, like telling a friend's story. Read each line as its own recording. The time marks show where each line plays in the video, so you know roughly how long it should take.

## How to record

- Quiet small room: a closet or a bedroom with a bed and curtains works best. No kitchens or bathrooms (echo).
- Phone voice recorder, held 15–20 cm (a hand's width) from your mouth, a little off to the side.
- Read each line 2–3 times in a row, pausing a couple of seconds between takes. Keep the best one.
- Calm and close, a little slower than normal. Lines 1–4 are quiet and a bit sad; from line 7 ("So we built ForkCast") lift your energy.
- Name the files `VO-01`, `VO-02`, … and send them to Viktor.

## Script

**VO-01** · 0:08 · *(soft)*
This is Maya. First semester at USC, first time living on her own.

**VO-02** · 0:13
She's halal, and she's allergic to sesame. And every night, dinner starts like this.

**VO-03** · 0:17
Three dining hall menus. Tiny allergen icons on every dish. And no way to tell which hall actually has something she can eat.

**VO-04** · 0:24
Guess wrong, and it's a twelve-minute walk to find out.

**VO-05** · 0:33 *(turn: more direct, it's the "this is real" moment)*
Maya is a character. Her problem isn't. Today, USC's three dining halls served two hundred seventy-one menu items, with six hundred four allergen and diet labels.

**VO-06** · 0:52
About one in ten U.S. adults has a food allergy. And for Maya, tonight, only eight of Parkside's twenty-three dinner dishes fit.

**VO-07** · 1:02 *(lift, confident)*
So we built ForkCast.

**VO-08** · 1:09
She answers a few taps: what she can't eat, what she loves, and where she lives.

**VO-09** · 1:16
ForkCast reads all three menus and removes every dish USC labels with something she can't eat.

**VO-10** · 1:23
In about four seconds, it tells her where to go: the Village, three minutes away, and what to put on her plate.

**VO-11** · 1:41 *(the most important line: slow down a little)*
AI understands the food, but it never makes an allergen decision. Code removes flagged dishes before the AI sees the menu, then checks every answer before she does. Two hundred and six automated tests fail if a flagged dish is ever recommended.

**VO-12** · 1:57 *(quicker, upbeat)*
It learns from every thumbs up, plans the whole week, knows when halls close, speaks seven languages, and emails her picks every morning.

**VO-13** · 2:14 *(warm)*
ForkCast can't cure homesickness. But nobody should skip dinner because the menu is a puzzle.

**VO-14** · 2:19 *(slow, short pause after "ForkCast.")*
ForkCast. Know before you go.
