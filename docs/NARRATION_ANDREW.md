# ForkCast video: narration script (Andrew)

Read each line as its own recording. The time marks show where each line plays in the video, so you know roughly how long it should take.

## How to record

- Quiet small room: a closet or a bedroom with a bed and curtains works best. No kitchens or bathrooms (echo).
- Phone voice recorder, held 15–20 cm (a hand's width) from your mouth, a little off to the side.
- Read each line 2–3 times in a row, pausing a couple of seconds between takes. Keep the best one.
- Relaxed, normal pace, like you're telling a friend. Smile a little; people can hear it.
- Name the files `VO-01`, `VO-02`, … and send them to Viktor.

## Script

**VO-01** · 0:16–0:32
Three times a day, every USC student asks the same question: where should I eat? If you're halal with a sesame allergy, that means opening three menus and decoding tiny icons on every dish.

**VO-02** · 0:32–0:40
We built ForkCast. Tell it once how you eat...

**VO-03** · 0:40–0:55
...in your own words. AI fills in your profile from USC's own labels: allergies, diet, what you love, where you live. Then swipe through a few real dishes from today's menus so it learns your taste.

**VO-04** · 0:55–1:10
And in seconds: your dining hall for this meal, how far you walk, and the dishes to get.

**VO-05** · 1:10–1:20
It compares all three halls, and shows how much of each menu actually fits you.

**VO-06** · 1:20–1:42 *(the most important line: slow down a little)*
Here's the important part. AI understands the food, but it never makes an allergen decision. Code removes anything USC labels with your allergens before the AI sees the menu, then checks every answer: real hall, real dishes, no flagged allergens, every label claim true. And it shows you what it hid, and why.

**VO-07** · 1:42–1:52
A different student gets a different answer.

**VO-08** · 1:52–2:10
It learns as you go. Thumbs up or down on any dish. Ate it? Tap "I ate this", rate it from one to ten, and the next student sees today's scores. It even avoids serving you the same thing all week.

**VO-09** · 2:10–2:22
Plan ahead with the whole week, and it won't send you to a hall that's about to close.

**VO-10** · 2:22–2:34
It speaks seven languages, has a dark mode, and can email you the day's picks every morning.

**VO-11** · 2:40–2:48 *(slow and confident, short pause after "ForkCast.")*
ForkCast. Know before you go.
