# Student survey (proof for the numbers in the video)

A Google Form we sent to USC students on **Nov 14, 2026**, the first day of the hackathon. `responses.csv` is the full export: **50 responses**.

**What the video says:** 27 of 50 students listed a dietary restriction. That's more than half.

**How we counted:** a response counts as a restriction if the answer to *"What are your dietary restrictions?"* is anything other than "none", "n/a" or a typo of those. The `has_restriction` column shows the call for every row, so anyone can recount. The 27 range from lactose intolerance and vegetarian to halal, no pork, kosher, and life-threatening allergies.

**Edits to the export:**
- Removed the name column. Students didn't agree to have their names published.
- One answer was "Beef" followed by a joke. We kept "Beef" (it counts as a restriction) and removed the joke. Without that row it's 26 of 49, still more than half.

Nothing else is changed. Other columns: year (39 freshmen, 3 sophomores, 4 juniors, 4 seniors) and how frustrated they are with the consistency of dining hall meals, from 1 to 10 (average 5.5).
