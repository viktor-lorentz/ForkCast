## 🖥️ The deck: record it once
1. Open [video-deck/index.html](video-deck/index.html) in Chrome and press `F` for fullscreen. Slide 0 is blank.
2. OBS: Display Capture, 1920×1080, 60 fps → **Start Recording**.
3. Press `A`. Every slide plays with the script's timing, about 2 minutes, hands-free. You can also step with `→`.
4. Stop and save as `DECK-graphics.mp4`.

**Keys:** `→`/`Space` next · `←` back · `R` replay · `A` autoplay · `N` notes (which line each slide goes with; turn them off before recording) · `F` fullscreen.

## ✂️ Putting it together in Descript
The full prompt to paste and the upload checklist are in [DESCRIPT_PROMPT.md](DESCRIPT_PROMPT.md).
1. New project. Drag in the voice files in order: O-01, O-02, O-03, O-04, O-05, A-01, O-06, A-02…A-07, O-07, A-08. Descript transcribes them, so the script becomes the timeline. Delete flubs by deleting words.
2. Drag in `DECK-graphics.mp4` and **Split** it into slides. Place each slide on its line using the **Deck slide** column above.
3. Put the filmed shots (A1–A9), app recordings (R02, R03) and existing footage (B02, B03, T1, T3) on their lines.
4. Keep T1 and T3 with their own audio.
5. Add music on its own track with **Ducking** under the voice. Let it swell at 1:16 (A8) and 2:11 (A9). Quiet piano for Part 1 and something lighter from 1:05 works (YouTube Audio Library, free).
6. **Captions → Add**, then fix any misheard words (*qabuli palaw*, *mantu*, *bolani*, *ForkCast*).
7. Apply **Studio Sound** to the voice tracks.
8. Export 1080p, 16:9.
